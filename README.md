# BIG-STORE-Customer-Behaviour-Analysis
A data analytics dashboard exploring customer purchasing behavior, seasonal sales trends, product category performance, and the impact of subscriptions on retail revenue.

Tools used: POWER BI FOR DASHBOARD 
/PYTHON FOR DATA CLEANING

<img width="1055" height="680" alt="87834" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/75cbb428-679c-4586-af08-1d6985fc0fa9" />

This project features an interactive data visualization dashboard designed to analyze customer behavior and sales performance for a retail store. The analysis breaks down purchasing patterns across product categories, seasonal variations, gender demographics, and subscription statuses to identify key revenue drivers and customer demographics.

Key Business Insights
Customer Base & Spending: The store has a base of 3.90K customers with an average purchase amount of $59.76 and a solid average review rating of 3.75.

Category Performance: Clothing is the dominant sales driver, generating $104K in revenue, followed by Accessories ($74K) and Footwear ($36K).

Subscription Impact: Non-subscribers currently account for the vast majority of sales revenue (73.12% / $170.44K), highlighting a potential area for subscription growth.

Demographics: Males make up the majority of the tracked customer base, accounting for 68% (2.65K) compared to females at 32% (1.25K).

Seasonal Consistency: Sales volume remains highly stable throughout the year, with a tight revenue range between $56K in the Summer and $60K in the Fall.
